# Branding & Visual Identity Guide: Lovina Ethical Marine

*Last updated: May 2026*
*Concept: Approachable, Ethical, Fun, and Crystal-Clear*

## 1. Brand Philosophy
We are a trusted, high-quality, and 100% ethical dolphin tour company in Lovina, North Bali. We are not a luxury resort. We are a team of friendly, local captains who care about wild dolphins and want our guests to have a fun, relaxing, and safe morning on the water.

## 2. Brand Voice & Rules of Simplicity
Our language must be **simple, friendly, and direct.** Many of our guests speak English as a second or third language (e.g. Russian, Chinese, German). If they have to search a word in a dictionary, or if they cannot easily picture the object, it is too vague.

### 🚫 What to Avoid (Overly Vague / "Quiet Luxury" Jargon):
* **Do NOT use pretentious or vague words** like *"The Quiet Encounter"*, *"Maritime Excellence"*, *"Acoustic Stress"*, or *"Integrated Brand"*. 
* **Do NOT use raw local terminology without explaining it.** Do not just write *"Private Outrigger"* (most travelers do not know what an "outrigger" is).

### ✅ What to Use (Clear, Fun, and Friendly):
* **Instead of "Private Outrigger"**, write: **"Your own private wooden boat (traditional Balinese outrigger boat with bamboo wings on the sides for perfect stability)"** or simply **"Private wooden boat"**.
* **Instead of "Acoustic stress"**, write: **"Loud engine noise that scares the dolphins."**
* **Instead of "Parallel approach"**, write: **"Driving slowly alongside the dolphins, never pointing the boat directly at them."**
* **Tone:** Fun, exciting, and happy. We are going to watch wild dolphins swim and play in the ocean! It is an adventure, not a solemn museum trip. Keep it warm, reliable, and friendly.

## 3. Color Palette: "Coastal Noir" (Approachable Premium)
We keep our clean, premium visual aesthetic (luminous off-white and deep indigo text) to show we are reliable and high-quality, but we pair it with friendly, simple copy:
* **Background:** Cloud Dancer (`#F0EEE9`) — Warm, welcoming off-white.
* **Primary Text:** Deep Indigo (`#1B2B32`) — High-contrast, easy-to-read dark blue-black.
* **Accent/Highlight:** Transformative Teal (`#23545B`) — Stable, nature-centric teal.
* **CTA Button:** Coral Pop (`#FF6347`) — Friendly, high-energy orange-red.

## 4. Simple Terms Glossary
* **Outrigger Boat:** Always explain it on the first mention: *"Traditional wooden outrigger boat (called a jukung, which has long bamboo floaters on the sides so it cannot tip over)."*
* **Ethical Rules:** Keep them simple:
  1. *We keep our distance* (never closer than 30 meters).
  2. *We turn off the engine* (or switch to neutral) when dolphins are close so they don't get scared by loud noise.
  3. *We never chase* or surround the pods.
