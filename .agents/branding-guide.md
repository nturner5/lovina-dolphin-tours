# Branding & Visual Identity Guide: Lovina Ethical Marine

*Last updated: 2026-05-26*
*Concept: "Integrated Marine Authority & Quiet Luxury"*

## 1. Brand Philosophy
Lovina Ethical Marine represents the definitive standard for marine encounters in North Bali. We are an integrated brand, not a directory. We move away from the "tour" commodity and toward "Marine Excellence." We evoke the calm of the open sea and the authority of a maritime institution.

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Tone:** Definitive, Professional, and Elite. We are the "Four Seasons" of the Lovina sea.
- **Style:** Editorial and minimal.
- **Key Phraseology:** "The Quiet Encounter," "Maritime Excellence," "Vetted Captains," "The Ethical Standard."

## 3. Color Palette: "Coastal Noir"
| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Visual Vibe |

## 4. Typography
- **Headings:** **Fraunces** (Bold/Semi-bold).
- **Body:** **Inter** (Regular/Bold).
