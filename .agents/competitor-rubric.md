# B2C Tour Booking Page: Competitor Benchmarking Rubric
*Benchmarked against Viator, GetYourGuide, and Klook for Lovina Dolphin Tours*

This rubric establishes the elite design, UX, and copywriting standards required to match or outperform the world's leading Online Travel Agencies (OTAs)—**Viator, GetYourGuide, and Klook**—while maintaining the premium, boutique "Coastal Noir" aesthetic of **Bali Dolphin Tours**.

---

## 📊 Benchmark Dimensions

### 1. Booking Flexibilities & Risk Reversal (The GetYourGuide/Viator Standard)
OTAs dominate conversions by minimizing the traveler's financial and planning anxiety. A premium boutique brand must offer equivalent or superior reassurance.

*   **24-Hour Free Cancellation:** Clearly stated directly near all CTAs.
*   **Flexible Weather Policy:** A clear plan for when the sea is too rough (e.g., free reschedule or 100% refund).
*   **Sighting Guarantee:** Since wildlife sightings are variable, a high-converting page must guarantee a free second trip or partial refund if dolphins aren't spotted.
*   **Reserve Now, Pay Later:** An expectation set early in the checkout flow.

### 2. Social Proof & Trust Cues (The GetYourGuide/Klook Standard)
Mass-market OTAs display thousands of reviews. A boutique site cannot match the sheer volume, but it can outperform on the **quality, transparency, and relevance** of its reviews.

*   **Verified Customer Testimonials:** Real traveler quotes mentioning specific benefits (e.g., "7:00 AM quietness," "no chasing," "hotel pickup was prompt").
*   **Granular Review Details:** Reviews should show traveler types (e.g., "Couple," "Family with Kids") to build relevance.
*   **Social Proof badges:** Integrating rating numbers (e.g., `★ 4.9`) with active, clickable badges or review grids.
*   **Real Media/Reels Grid:** Raw, unedited video proof of the dolphin encounter and snorkeling directly on the page to validate authenticity.

### 3. Information Architecture & Scannability (The Viator Standard)
Travelers scan tour pages to quickly extract logistics. Viator excels at placing key details in high-visibility bullet grids.

*   **At-a-Glance Tour Grid:** Quick-reference boxes for **Duration**, **Start Time**, **Group Type** (Private), and **Inclusions**.
*   **Visual Hourly Itinerary:** A timeline that walks through the experience step-by-step (e.g., 07:00 departure ➔ 08:00 dolphin watching ➔ 09:00 snorkeling ➔ 10:00 return).
*   **Explicit Exclusions & What to Bring:** Clear lists of what is *not* included (e.g., towels, sunscreen) to manage expectations and reduce post-tour disputes.

### 4. Mobile Checkout UX (The Klook Standard)
Over 80% of activity bookings in Bali occur on mobile viewports. The checkout flow must feel native and frictionless.

*   **Mobile-Sticky Bottom Bar:** A persistent bottom bar showing the calculated total and a direct "Book Now" CTA, keeping the purchase path within thumb reach.
*   **Thumb-Friendly Inputs:** Large date picker and guest count selectors (minimum tap target of `54px`).
*   **Minimized Typing:** Pre-selected add-ons, auto-formatted phone numbers (with country codes), and minimal text fields.

## 📝 Competitor Rubric Scoring (Out of 100 Points)

Use this scoring rubric to evaluate and audit our tour landing and checkout pages:

### 1. Headline & Value Proposition Clarity (20 Points)
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Can a user understand the "Anti-Sunrise" (7:00 AM / No-Chase) benefit within 5 seconds?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is it clear that this is a *private* boat charter, not a shared tour?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is the contrast between our peaceful tours and the chaotic 6:00 AM chase immediately obvious?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Does the hero section have a clear, single-focus call to action?

### 2. Trust & Verified Social Proof (20 Points)
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Are real customer reviews (text + traveler type) displayed on the page?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Are rating badges (e.g., `★ 4.9`) backed by verified platform badges or source links?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is there a live/interactive video grid (Instagram Reels) showing real footage of the dolphin encounters?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is our wildlife-friendly "Behavioral Contract" (neutral engines, parallel approach) clearly displayed?

### 3. Activity Logistics & Scannability (20 Points)
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is there an "At a Glance" grid summarizing: Duration, Start Time, Private Group, Languages?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is there an hour-by-hour itinerary timeline showing exactly what happens?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is the "What to Bring" section highly visible (reef booties, sunscreen, towel)?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Are exact pickup zones and meeting points (with Google Maps coordinates) detailed?

### 4. Risk Reversal & Guarantees (20 Points)
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is the `85% Sighting Guarantee` explicitly displayed near the final checkout CTA?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is a clear weather-cancellation policy stated before payment?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is the refund policy (e.g., "100% refund for cancellations up to 24h before") visible in the checkout flow?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is secure payment processing (Stripe / SSL) visibly guaranteed?

### 5. Mobile Checkout Performance & AOV (20 Points)
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Is the Mobile-Sticky Bottom Booking Bar active and showing the live total?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Are pickup add-ons ($42 Ubud / $60 Canggu) easily selected via simple checkboxes?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Does the checkout page avoid unnecessary intermediate loading screens or redirects?
*   [ ] **(5 pts)** Are form errors highlighted dynamically with clear, localized instructions?
