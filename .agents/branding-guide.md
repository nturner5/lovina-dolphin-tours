# Branding & Visual Identity Guide: Lovina Ethical (V2)

*Last updated: 2026-05-26*
*Concept: "Eco-Action & High-Contrast Coastal"*

## 1. Brand Philosophy
Our brand has evolved from "Warm Minimalism" to **"Regenerative High-Contrast."** While we still value the calm of the North Bali Sea, we now anchor that calm with deep, authoritative tones and vibrant, energetic accents. We are the **premium, professional** choice for ethical tourism.

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Tone:** Authoritative, Expert, and Decisive. We don't just "suggest" ethics; we **guarantee** them.
- **Style:** Editorial and premium. Think *National Geographic* meets *Monocle Magazine*.
- **Key Phraseology:** "The Ocean’s Quietest Encounter," "Vetted Captains," "Guaranteed Respect," "Beyond the Sunrise Mob."

## 3. Color Palette: "V2 Coastal Noir"
We use a high-contrast palette to ensure the site feels modern, professional, and has clear action paths.

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Visual Vibe |
|------|------------|----------|-------------|
| **Primary Background** | Cloud Dancer | `#F0EEE9` | A luminous, warm off-white that feels premium and breathable. |
| **Primary Text / Anchor** | Deep Indigo | `#1B2B32` | A near-black blue. Signals deep water, lava stone, and professional authority. |
| **Action / Authority** | Transformative Teal | `#23545B` | A rich blue-green. Used for secondary buttons, borders, and brand accents. |
| **The "Hook" (CTA)** | Coral Pop | `#FF6347` | A vibrant, high-contrast orange. Used exclusively for primary "Book Now" buttons and key alerts. |
| **Status / Success** | Sage Leaf | `#929C92` | Used for "Vetted" badges and sustainability-related items. |

## 4. Typography: "The Trusted Reporter"
We mix a modern, grounded serif for trust and heritage with a clean sans-serif for action.

- **Headings (H1, H2, H3):** **Fraunces** (Google Font).
  - *Why:* It has a "soft serif" quality that feels human but has enough weight to look authoritative and "high-end journal."
- **Body & UI:** **Inter** (Regular to Bold weights).
  - *Why:* The gold standard for digital usability and clarity.

## 5. Visual Identity Elements
- **Aggressive Radii:** Use very rounded corners (`rounded-[3rem]`) to mimic the organic flow of water and smooth river stones.
- **High-Contrast Depth:** Use deep shadows (`shadow-2xl`) against light backgrounds to create a tactile, layered feel.
- **Cinematic Anchor:** Hero sections must lead with high-quality, high-contrast video/imagery. No faded or "washed out" filters.
- **The "Ethical Seal":** A premium circular badge using the **Sage Leaf** color.

## 6. Iconography
- **Style:** Thin-line, custom-feeling icons (1px stroke).
- **Placement:** Always paired with generous whitespace.
