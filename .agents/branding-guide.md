# Branding & Visual Identity Guide: Lovina Ethical Dolphin Tours

*Last updated: 2026-05-26*
*Concept: "Quiet Marine Sanctuary"*

## 1. Brand Philosophy
Our brand is built on **"Warm Minimalism."** We avoid the high-energy "adventure" cliches of bright teals and loud fonts. Instead, we evoke the feeling of the North Bali sea at 8:00 AM—glassy, calm, and respectful. We are the "Anti-Crowd" choice.

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone
*   **Tone:** Expert but humble, inviting, and deeply respectful of marine life.
*   **Style:** Clean and direct. We "whisper" our authority rather than shouting it.
*   **Key Phraseology:** "The Relaxed Encounter," "Beyond the Sunrise," "Quiet Observation," "Vetted Captains."
*   **Avoid:** "Chase," "Racing," "Cheap," "Best Price," "Guaranteed Sighting" (use "Highest Sighting Probability" instead).

## 3. Color Palette: "The North Bali Shore"
We use an atmospheric, nature-inspired palette that blurs the lines between the sea and the volcanic sand.

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Visual Vibe |
|------|------------|----------|-------------|
| **Primary Background** | Soft Bone | `#F4F1EB` | Warm, organic off-white (never pure white). |
| **Deep Accents** | Volcanic Navy | `#1A292F` | The deep Bali Sea and lava stone. Use for headings. |
| **Action / Links** | Muted Slate | `#6B8E99` | The color of the water in the shade. |
| **Secondary BG** | Sand Dune | `#DEBDA6` | Warmth and texture. Use for call-outs or dividers. |
| **Status / Badges** | Sage Leaf | `#929C92` | Sustainability and "Vetted" status. |

## 4. Typography: "The Hybrid Editorial"
We mix a high-contrast serif for a premium, trustworthy "storytelling" feel with a clean sans-serif for modern usability.

*   **Headings (H1, H2, H3):** **Cormorant Garamond** (or similar elegant Serif). 
    *   *Why:* Evokes heritage, craft, and "Quiet Luxury."
*   **Body & UI:** **Inter** or **Montserrat** (Light to Regular weights).
    *   *Why:* Ensures high legibility for booking flows and technical details.
*   **Accents / Captions:** **Cormorant Garamond Italic**.
    *   *Why:* Adds a "hand-signed" or editorial touch to testimonials and quotes.

## 5. Visual Identity Elements
*   **Soft Shapes:** Use rounded corners (e.g., `rounded-2xl` in Tailwind) and arched framing for images to avoid harsh, aggressive angles.
*   **Textural Depth:** Incorporate subtle background textures that mimic handmade paper or a light "limewash" grain to add sensory depth to the screen.
*   **Cinematic Video:** Use slow-motion, color-graded drone or underwater footage. Avoid fast cuts or "vlogger-style" transitions.
*   **The "Vetted" Seal:** A clean, minimal circular badge in **Sage Leaf** (`#929C92`) that appears on every captain's profile.

## 6. Iconography
*   **Style:** Minimalist, thin-line icons (e.g., Lucide or Phosphor Icons).
*   **Weight:** Keep icons light (`1px` or `1.5px` stroke) to maintain the airy, premium feel.
